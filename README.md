Online Learning Engagement and Student Performance Analysis is a
Power BI-based data analytics project developed to understand how
students interact with an online learning platform and how engagement
relates to academic outcomes.

The analysis covers 22,422 students across 13 regions of the UK and
Ireland. It examines platform activity, engagement levels, average
scores, final results, dropout patterns, risk levels, education
background, gender, and regional differences.

The project is designed to support course designers, academic leaders,
and student-support teams in identifying patterns that may require early
intervention.
